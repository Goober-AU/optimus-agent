- Added `OCC3_MAX_TURN_SECONDS`: a per-boot opt-in that widens the native OCC-3 turn admission window (default 300s) for deployments serving armed capture turns. Live TS13 evidence showed a thinking-heavy armed turn legitimately decoding past 300s dying server-side with `deadline_exceeded`/504 mid-generation; deployments opt in per boot, the default stays 300s, explicit caller profiles are never overridden, and a declared-but-invalid value fails closed at install.
