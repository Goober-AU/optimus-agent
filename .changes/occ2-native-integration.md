Added: native OCC-2 symbolic encoding for agent-to-agent message bodies, behind a new `agentCommunication.occ2Enabled` settings flag that defaults to OFF (research/integration/OCC2-INTEGRATION-DESIGN.md, Phase 6). When enabled, structured OCC-1 contract bodies sent through `agent_message.send` (direct and broadcast arms) and the RLM child-spawn prompt are dual-encoded with `occ2::encode_dual` (symbolic dialect section, `#FALLBACK-OCC1` sentinel, full OCC-1 text): recipients see the symbolic section in the prompt while the durable transcript record stores the dual payload verbatim, so `occ1_fallback` reconstructs the typed payload after daemon restarts even with no dialect cards loaded. A size guard sends plain OCC-1 when the dual form would exceed the 16,384-char transport bound (never truncates), any encode error passes the body through unchanged, and deltas are excluded from wrapping in v1. Off means byte-identical OCC-0/OCC-1: queueing, receipts, rate limits, roster and prompt-id framing are untouched, `AgentSessionMessageAgentSummary` gains an optional `occ_capability` field (absent = OCC-1), and telemetry gains a strict `Symbolic` classify arm that fires only when `occ2::decode` succeeds on the symbolic section (an OCC/2 body consumed through its fallback reports Occ1; the pinned "OCC/2\nSYMBOL=x" case stays Text). Includes a pure three-strike ledger (`Occ2StrikeLedger`) and a strict-then-fallback decode helper for tier-3 session pair disable, plus the offline `occ2_comm_fidelity` suite (12 tests) over the 69-message `research/tasks/msgs_corpus.json` with 18 declared holdout rows: dual round trips, unknown-dialect/hash-mismatch/corruption fault injection, sentinel split, size guard, negotiation absence, durability and compaction-survival round trips.

Deferred follow-ups (owner: parent integration): daemon-side `OccCapability::here()` advertisement in `create_agent_message_agent_summary` (daemon_mode.rs, where this change adds only `occ_capability: None` at the two full-field struct literals) and the passive-RLM summary literal; the runtime recorder that feeds `Occ2StrikeLedger` from actual decode incidents; evolved-dialect card loading at session start; model-pair and task-family gating on `DialectCard` metadata.
